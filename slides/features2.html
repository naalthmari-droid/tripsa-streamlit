Key Features · 2/2

Built for groups — a first in the market.

Collaborative planning room
Each trip gets a shared room with a short, shareable invite code — members join in seconds.
Voting & live consensus score
Members enter preferences and vote on destinations; a group-consensus score picks the winning route.
Comments & discussion
Per-trip or per-destination threads keep the whole group on the same page.
"For You" recommendations
Personalized suggestions that learn from past trips — with star ratings & reviews feeding back into the engine.
Email notifications
Alerts on booking confirmation, member joins, and trip updates keep everyone informed.
Why it matters
Group travel is the dominant domestic segment in Saudi Arabia — TRIPSA is the first tool built to plan it together.
