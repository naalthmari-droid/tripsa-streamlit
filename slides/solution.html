The Solution

TRIPSA: an intelligent route engine for Saudi road trips.

01
Optimized route engine
Builds an optimized route between cities (TSP + 2-opt) with distance, drive time, cost and per-stop stay dates.
02
Two ways to plan
"Pick cities myself" (custom) or "Use a certified route" — STA-approved routes brought to life.
03
Group-planning room
Every trip gets a shared room with a short, shareable invite code for collaborative planning.
04
Learning recommendations
Personalized "For You" suggestions that improve from past trips and user ratings.
