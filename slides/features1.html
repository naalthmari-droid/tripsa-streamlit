Key Features · 1 of 2

Engineered for the traveler's real journey.

1
Route optimization engine
Orders cities to minimize drive time (TSP + 2-opt), with per-stop arrival/departure dates and nights.
2
Smart day scheduling
Plans each day around the traveler's wake/sleep hours and pace — relaxed, moderate or action-packed.
3
Real local data
Actual attractions and restaurants per city, matched to the traveler's favorite cuisine and accommodation.
4
Interactive route map
Numbered stops and the path between them, rendered on a live map.
